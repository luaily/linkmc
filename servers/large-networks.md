# Large networks

LinkMC runs its own pages for the largest public Minecraft networks, such as Hypixel and
DonutSMP. You can find them under
[Servers → Large networks](https://linkmc.app/servers/networks).

A large network page is for status and [rich presence](rich-presence.md). It isn't managed
by the network itself.

## What a network page shows

- A short description of the network, and a link to its website.
- The address that players use to connect.
- Whether the network is online, how many players are on it, and its uptime and player
  history on the network's status page.
- An **Anarchy · unmoderated** label for anarchy servers.

The page's icon comes from the network's own website.

Large networks don't have owners, staff, members, applications, or bumps on LinkMC. To
manage your own listing with your staff, [list your server](server-listing.md) instead.

### How LinkMC checks the status

LinkMC pings each network every five minutes. Some networks block pings from LinkMC. For
those networks, the player count comes from LinkMC players who are on the network at the
time, through the LinkMC mod.

## Rich presence

Players who use the LinkMC mod show **Playing on** and the network's name, with its address
and icon, in their Discord and Fluxer status and on their LinkMC profile. The address that
LinkMC shows is always the network's main address, not the one the player typed.

Any address on the network's domain counts. For example, `mc.hypixel.net` and
`hypixel.net` both show as Hypixel. Servers hosted on Minehut, at `*.minehut.gg`, show as
Minehut unless they're listed on LinkMC themselves.

A network can ask LinkMC to stop naming it in rich presence. Email
`development@linkmc.app`. Players on it then show **Multiplayer**.

## Requirements

A large network must meet every requirement:

- **Code of conduct:** It follows the [Servers Code of Conduct](server-code-of-conduct.md).
- **Size:** At least 1,500 players online on average each day. LinkMC checks this over several
  weeks.
- **Open to everyone:** It isn't region-locked, and it isn't only for players in Russia.
  It also isn't hosted in Russia, because many players can't reach servers there because of
  internet provider blocks and sanctions. Networks for Eastern European or Russian-speaking
  players are welcome when they're hosted elsewhere.
- **Not cracked-only:** Players with a paid Minecraft account can join. Networks that also
  accept unofficial accounts can apply.
- **Not gambling or pay-to-win:** The network isn't built around gambling, or around buying an
  advantage over other players.
- **Free to join:** Players don't pay to get in.
- **Anarchy, labeled:** Anarchy and unmoderated servers are welcome, but they must say so
  in their description.

LinkMC can make exceptions to the size requirement for networks with lasting importance to the
community, such as the oldest anarchy servers or official event servers.

A network that falls below the requirements for a long time can be removed, so that another
network can take its place.

## Apply

1. Sign in to LinkMC.
1. Go to [Servers → Large networks](https://linkmc.app/servers/networks), and then click
   **Apply as a network**.
1. Enter the network's name, address, website, average daily players, and a short
   description.
1. If the network is an anarchy or unmoderated server, select the anarchy option, and say so in
   the description.
1. Confirm each requirement, and then click **Send application**.

You can see your application's status on the same page. If LinkMC turns it down, the
reason appears there. You can have one application waiting at a time.

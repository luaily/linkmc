# Servers Code of Conduct

LinkMC lists Minecraft servers so that players can find good places to play. This code of
conduct sets out what LinkMC expects from every server and network on it. Most of it is
common sense: be honest, be fair, and keep players safe.

The code applies to:

- Every server listed on LinkMC, and its owner and staff when they act for the server.
- Every [large network](large-networks.md) that LinkMC lists.
- Discord and Fluxer communities linked to a listing, where they speak for the server.

Breaking the spirit of a rule counts the same as breaking its letter.

## Be honest about your server

- Describe your server accurately: its name, address, game modes, and what players can do there.
- Don't pretend to be another server, network, creator, or brand, or suggest that you're
  connected to one when you aren't.
- Don't inflate your player count, uptime, or reviews.
- If your server is an anarchy or unmoderated server, say so in its description. LinkMC labels
  anarchy servers so that players know what to expect.

## Keep it free to join

- No paid entry. Players must be able to join and play without paying, including through
  "whitelist fees" or paid access codes.
- No real-money gambling, including selling chances to win items or ranks for real money.
- Follow the [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines), which
  cover what servers may sell.

## No botting

- Don't use bots or alternate accounts to inflate your player count or your server-list
  status.
- Don't bump, vote, join, or apply automatically, or ask players to make extra LinkMC accounts
  to do it.
- Don't create LinkMC accounts or communities that only exist to promote your server.

## No activity scraping

- Don't scrape LinkMC: player profiles, rich presence, member lists, or server pages. Use the
  [LinkMC API](api-reference.md) within its [terms](api-terms.md).
- Don't record what players do on other servers, or build lists of where players play, from
  LinkMC or anywhere else.
- Don't use bots that join other servers to collect their player lists or chat.

## Keep players safe

LinkMC has zero tolerance for harassment. Don't harass, threaten, intimidate, or stalk anyone,
on your server or off it, and don't let your staff do it.

Harassment includes demeaning comments, images, or behavior aimed at people because of their:

- Race, ethnicity, nationality, citizenship, or native language
- Sex, gender identity, or gender expression
- Sexual orientation
- Physical or mental disability
- Appearance or body size
- Age
- Religion or beliefs, including having none
- Location or socioeconomic status

Also:

- Don't publish anyone's personal information, such as their real name, address, IP address,
  or photos, without their permission.
- Don't allow sexual content involving minors, in any form. LinkMC reports it to the
  authorities.
- Keep listings suitable for LinkMC's audience, which includes players aged 13 and older.

Anarchy servers can be unmoderated in-game, but the rules about personal information, minors,
and threats of real-world harm still apply to them.

## Respect players' privacy

- Collect only the player data that your server needs, and don't sell it.
- Don't share players' IP addresses, or use them for anything but running and protecting your
  server.
- Respect players' choices on LinkMC. If a player hides a membership or their rich presence,
  don't reveal it.

## Play fair on security

- Don't distribute malware, or ask players to install anything that harms their computer or
  account.
- Don't phish. Never ask players for their Microsoft password, or to sign in on a page that
  isn't Microsoft's.
- Don't sell or hand out stolen or shared Minecraft accounts.
- Don't attack, crash, or overload other servers, or LinkMC.

## Follow the law and Minecraft's rules

Follow the [Minecraft End User License Agreement](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/eula), the Minecraft
Usage Guidelines, and the laws that apply to you and your players.

## Report a problem

If you see a server or network breaking this code, email `development@linkmc.app`. Include:

- The server's name and its LinkMC page, such as `https://linkmc.app/servers/12`.
- What happened, and when.
- Screenshots or other evidence, if you have them.

You can report anonymously, but then LinkMC can't ask you questions or tell you what
happened. To report a security problem, email the same address and don't post it in public.

LinkMC is run by one person, so replies can take a few days.

## What happens after a report

LinkMC reads every report, and might ask the server's owner for their side. What happens next
depends on how serious the problem is and how it affected players:

1. A note to the owner explaining what to fix.
1. Removing parts of the listing, such as its description, icon, or tags.
1. Suspending the listing, so that it no longer appears on LinkMC.
1. Removing the server, and not letting its owner list servers again.

For serious problems, LinkMC can suspend a listing while it looks into a report. A problem in
a linked community can affect the server's listing.

## Appeals

To appeal a decision, email `development@linkmc.app` within 60 days. Say which decision you're
appealing and why it should change.

## Changes to this code

LinkMC reviews this code at least once a year, and after incidents that show it's missing
something. Changes are announced in [News](https://linkmc.app/announcements).
